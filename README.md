Developed an IoT-enabled wearable device designed to detect patient falls in real time and immediately notify caregivers or nursing staff.
The system uses motion and orientation sensors to identify abnormal movement patterns associated with falls and automatically
triggers alerts to enable faster response during emergencies.

The device was designed with a focus on patient safety, continuous monitoring, low-power operation, and real-time communication.
Through this project, I gained experience in embedded systems development, sensor integration, IoT communication,
wearable technology design, prototyping, testing, and healthcare-focused product development.
